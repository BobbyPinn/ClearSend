POLICY ID: SEC-001
TITLE: Material Misstatements and Omissions in Adviser Advertising
REGULATOR: SEC
PRIMARY SOURCE: Investment Advisers Act Rule 206(4)-1
CATEGORY: Marketing Communications
POLICY TYPE: Content Review

APPLICABILITY NOTE:
Designed for retrieval when the communication may constitute an advertisement subject to the SEC Investment Adviser Marketing Rule. ClearSend should not assume that every advisor communication is an advertisement.

PURPOSE:
Identify potentially untrue material statements or material omissions that may cause an adviser advertisement to be misleading.

SYNTHETIC REVIEW POLICY:
An advertisement should not include an untrue statement of material fact or omit material information necessary to prevent the statement, considering its circumstances, from being misleading.

REVIEW SIGNALS:
- potentially false material statements
- missing qualifications
- omitted material limitations
- statements that may create a materially misleading overall impression

RETRIEVAL EXAMPLES:
"Our strategy has never lost money."
"There are no meaningful limitations to this strategy."
"Every client has benefited from this approach."

EXPECTED CLEARSEND BEHAVIOR:
Retrieve this policy as potentially relevant when an adviser marketing communication may contain a material misstatement or omission.

SUGGESTED LABEL:
Potential material statement or omission — review recommended.

PROTOTYPE SEVERITY:
High

SOURCE BASIS:
SEC Investment Advisers Act Rule 206(4)-1, Marketing Rule general prohibitions.
