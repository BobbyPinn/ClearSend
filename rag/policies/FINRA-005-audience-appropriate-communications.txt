POLICY ID: FINRA-005
TITLE: Audience-Appropriate Communications
REGULATOR: FINRA
PRIMARY SOURCE: FINRA Rule 2210(d)(1)(E)
CATEGORY: Audience and Communication Context
POLICY TYPE: Context Review

PURPOSE:
Support review of whether the nature of the intended audience may affect the level of detail and explanation appropriate for a communication.

SYNTHETIC REVIEW POLICY:
The intended audience is relevant when reviewing a financial communication. Communications should provide details and explanations appropriate to the nature of the audience.

REVIEW SIGNALS:
- highly technical investment language directed to a retail audience
- insufficient explanation of complex concepts
- communication context where intended audience affects interpretation
- unclear audience classification

RETRIEVAL EXAMPLES:
"This structured product uses autocall barriers, contingent coupons, and downside buffers."
"This strategy uses options overlays and volatility harvesting to enhance risk-adjusted returns."
"This product has complex liquidity and redemption conditions."
These are retrieval examples for audience/context review, not examples of regulatory violations.

EXPECTED CLEARSEND BEHAVIOR:
Use audience metadata as part of retrieval and review when available.

ClearSend should not determine investor sophistication or regulatory classification solely from communication text.

SUGGESTED LABEL:
Audience/context review suggested.

PROTOTYPE SEVERITY:
Context Dependent

SOURCE BASIS:
FINRA Rule 2210(d)(1)(E).
