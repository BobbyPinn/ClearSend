POLICY ID: FINRA-004
TITLE: Material Information, Omissions, and Context
REGULATOR: FINRA
PRIMARY SOURCE: FINRA Rule 2210(d)(1)(A)
CATEGORY: Material Information
POLICY TYPE: Content Review

PURPOSE:
Identify communications where omitted material facts or qualifications may cause the communication to be misleading.

SYNTHETIC REVIEW POLICY:
Financial communications should provide a sound basis for evaluating relevant facts. A material fact or qualification should not be omitted when its omission, considering the context of the communication, could make the communication misleading.

REVIEW SIGNALS:
- important qualifications appear to be missing
- investment benefits are stated without important conditions
- statements depend on assumptions that are not communicated
- material limitations appear to be omitted
- selective information may change the overall impression

RETRIEVAL EXAMPLES:
"There are no fees you need to worry about."
"This investment gives you immediate access to your money."
"This provides tax-free income."
"This product protects your principal."

EXPECTED CLEARSEND BEHAVIOR:
Retrieve this policy when the communication may require additional context, qualifications, or material information.

ClearSend should not independently determine whether an omitted fact is legally material.

SUGGESTED LABEL:
Additional context or qualification may be needed.

PROTOTYPE SEVERITY:
Medium
