POLICY ID: SEC-004
TITLE: Fair and Balanced Presentation of Specific Investment Advice
REGULATOR: SEC
PRIMARY SOURCE: Investment Advisers Act Rule 206(4)-1
CATEGORY: Specific Investment Advice
POLICY TYPE: Content Review

APPLICABILITY NOTE:
Designed for communications potentially subject to the SEC Investment Adviser Marketing Rule.

PURPOSE:
Identify adviser advertisements referencing specific investment advice that may require review for fair and balanced presentation.

SYNTHETIC REVIEW POLICY:
References to specific investment advice in an advertisement should be presented in a fair and balanced manner.

REVIEW SIGNALS:
- highlighting successful investment recommendations without appropriate context
- selective presentation of specific investment advice
- discussion of winning recommendations that may create an unbalanced impression
- presentation of particular investment selections without balanced context

RETRIEVAL EXAMPLES:
"Look at these five stocks we recommended — every one was a winner."
"Our technology picks made clients significant money."
"These recommendations show why our investment process works."

EXPECTED CLEARSEND BEHAVIOR:
Retrieve this policy when an advertisement references specific investment advice and may warrant review for fair and balanced presentation.

SUGGESTED LABEL:
Specific investment advice presentation — review suggested.

PROTOTYPE SEVERITY:
Medium to High

SOURCE BASIS:
SEC Investment Adviser Marketing Rule general prohibitions concerning specific investment advice.
