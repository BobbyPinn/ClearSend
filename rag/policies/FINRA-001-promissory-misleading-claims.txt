POLICY ID: FINRA-001
TITLE: False, Exaggerated, Unwarranted, Promissory, or Misleading Claims
REGULATOR: FINRA
PRIMARY SOURCE: FINRA Rule 2210(d)(1)(B)
CATEGORY: Communications Content Standards
POLICY TYPE: Content Review

PURPOSE:
Identify financial communications containing language that may be false, exaggerated, unwarranted, promissory, or misleading.

SYNTHETIC REVIEW POLICY:
Communications should be reviewed when they state or imply uncertain investment outcomes as certain, make unsupported absolute claims, exaggerate benefits, or otherwise create a potentially misleading impression.

REVIEW SIGNALS:
- guaranteed investment success
- certainty of profit
- statements that an investor cannot lose money
- exaggerated claims regarding an investment or strategy
- absolute claims about future investment outcomes
- statements that could create a misleading impression

RETRIEVAL EXAMPLES:
"This investment is guaranteed to make you money."
"You can't lose with this fund."
"This strategy is guaranteed to outperform."
"There is absolutely no way this investment can go down."
"This is definitely going to be a winning investment."
